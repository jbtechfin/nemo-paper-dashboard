# Pre-registered forward PAPER trial: FX relative-volatility basket

Registered 2026-10-18 (Asia/Calcutta). This file was committed before the first paper trade.
Do not change the rules below while the trial runs. Any change ends this trial and needs a new pre-registration.

**PAPER ONLY.** Offline simulated book: no broker, no exchange, no API keys, no live order path of any kind.

## Basis
- Research: `reports/research/A_fx_relvol.md`, best config "cv N=20, R=21".
- Backtest 2010–2026: Sharpe 0.80, CAGR 3.2%, max DD 5.9%, 14/17 positive years.
- Multiple testing: BH-adjusted p 0.024, deflated Sharpe 0.93 (borderline).
- Expectation going in: Sharpe roughly 0.3–0.8 at best; zero edge is a live possibility.

## Fixed parameters
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Start date | 2026-10-18 |
| Starting equity | 100,000 paper USD |
| Universe (10) | EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, USDCAD, USDCHF, NZDUSD, EURJPY, GBPJPY, EURGBP (Yahoo symbols `XXXYYY=X`) |
| Signal | cv20 = std(Close, 20 daily closes) / mean(Close, 20 daily closes), per pair, completed daily bars only |
| Direction | +1, fixed (validated): LONG the 3 pairs with the HIGHEST cv20, SHORT the 3 with the LOWEST. No refitting during the trial |
| Weights | +1/6 of equity per long, −1/6 per short; gross 1.0, net 0, no leverage beyond gross 1 |
| Rebalance | At the close of the first trading day of each calendar month. The initial entry is at the latest completed close on the first run. Between rebalances positions drift with prices (no top-ups) |
| Costs | 1 bp per side on traded notional |
| P&L model | Return-based: a position with USD notional n earns n × (Close_t / Close_{t−1} − 1) per day. The quote-currency conversion of P&L and swap/carry are ignored, as in the backtest |
| Ties / missing | Rank with ties broken by symbol order. A rebalance needs a valid close for all 10 pairs on that date |

## Data rules
- **Source:** yfinance daily closes. Fallback: Frankfurter (ECB reference rates, crosses derived), which is used only when Yahoo fails. The source of every processed date is logged.
- **Completed bars only:** weekend-dated bars are ignored, and only dates before the current UTC date are used.
- **Stale data:**
  - If the newest date with all 10 closes is more than 4 calendar days older than the last expected weekday, the data is STALE. Positions are not marked forward, no trades happen, and the issue is logged.
  - Missing pairs on the newest date mean that date is not processed yet.
  - A due rebalance that cannot run stays pending and executes on the next good date.
- **Suspicious ticks:**
  - A move above 2.5% on the newest date is held for one day for confirmation.
  - If it reverses the next day (spike and revert, the same filter as the research), that close is replaced by the previous close.

## Monitoring and stop rules
- **Drawdown stop:** after each daily mark, if drawdown from peak equity is above 8%, flatten everything at that close (paying costs) and set status HALTED. No further trades.
- **Reviews:**
  - Reviews happen at 6 months (first processed date on or after 2027-04-18) and 12 months (on or after 2027-10-18).
  - At each review, compute the annualised Sharpe of daily paper returns over the trailing 126 trading days, or since start if shorter.
  - If it is below 0, flatten and set HALTED.
- **End:** the trial concludes at the 12-month review (status COMPLETED if not halted). Continuing needs a new pre-registration.
- **Not a success criterion:** a positive review only means "not stopped". Judge against the expectation above, not against hope.

## Files
- `scripts/fx_trial_daily.py`: the only process that changes trial state. It is idempotent, can run several times a day, and uses a lock file.
- `paper/fx_trial/state.json`: positions, peak, status, last processed date.
- `paper/fx_trial/ledger.csv`: every paper trade.
- `paper/fx_trial/equity.csv`: daily marks.
- `paper/fx_trial/prices.csv`: every close actually used, with its source.
- `paper/fx_trial/runs.log`: a line per run, including data issues.
- `paper/fx_trial/status.md`: human summary.

## Why this rule
Source: `reports/research/crypto_daily_study.md` and `reports/research/crypto_daily_selection.json`.

**Selection rule, stated before looking at the holdout:**
- The SMA lookbacks whose neighbourhood OOS Sharpe (adjacent lookbacks, daily and weekly checks) is at least 0.80 form the robust plateau. That is 50d and 100d.
- Inside the plateau, pick the variant with the smallest OOS max drawdown.
- Result: **SMA100, weekly check**.

**OOS 2021-01-01 → 2026-06-17**, after 15 bps/side + 2 bps slippage:

| | SMA100 weekly | BTC buy and hold |
|---|---|---|
| Sharpe | 0.97 | 0.54 |
| CAGR | 36.7% | 15.8% |
| Max drawdown | 34% | 77% |
| Turnover | 5.7x/yr | – |
| Positive years | 4 of 6 | – |
| Holdout (2026-06-18 → 2026-10-17) | +17.3% | +26.3% |

**Statistics:**
- Deflated Sharpe vs zero: 0.96.
- **Versus buy and hold, the excess (+8%/yr) is NOT statistically significant**: p = 0.32, BH-adjusted 0.68.

**Honest expectation:** the rule's demonstrated value is drawdown control: it sits in cash during long bear markets, as in 2022. It is not proven to beat holding BTC. It can lag in strong rallies and lose in whipsaws (2026 YTD before the holdout: Sharpe −2.5).

## Origin and evidence
- Study: `reports/research/portfolio/study.md` (code `research/portfolio/portfolio_study.py`, `sleeves.py`; commits 76ee8bc / a164a4d).
- Of six candidate sleeves, four had a base variant passing Benjamini-Hochberg (q = 0.10) on the pre-holdout window:
  **fxvol, btctrend, xmom, fundcarry**. FX carry and multi-asset trend failed and are excluded.
- Backtest of risk parity on these four ("rp_real_only"), 10% vol target, caps and brakes:
  pre-holdout 2021-01-01..2026-06-17 Sharpe 1.62, 14.8%/yr, vol 8.8%, max DD 10.9%, longest underwater 307 days;
  holdout 2026-06-18..10-17 Sharpe 3.0 (+7.7% in 122 days); every calendar year 2021-2026 positive (2022: +1.8%).
- **Realistic expectation for this trial: Sharpe 0.6-1.0, about 5-9% a year, drawdowns of 5-10% are normal.** The backtest numbers are
  an upper bound: fxvol, btctrend and xmom were picked on data overlapping the pre-holdout window (only the 4-month holdout is clean
  for them), the funding-carry sleeve sits on its 0.5 cap almost all the time, and the crypto sleeves have only ~6 years of history.

## Capital and notional
- **$10,000 paper USD, a separate account** from the FX trial ($100k), the BTC trial ($10k) and the crypto momentum trial ($10k).
  Nothing is shared or netted between trials.
- The study had **no dollar notional**: it works in daily returns per $1 of capital and its weights are capital multiples. Any starting
  capital scales the same way, so $10,000 was chosen to match the other crypto trials. The October weights sum to ~2.49x capital
  (mostly the FX sleeve, 1.61x), i.e. the book uses margin/leverage as FX and perp accounts allow. Paper only.

## Portfolio construction (exact study engine, `build(method="rp")`)
- **Risk parity (equal risk contribution)** on the annualised covariance (×365) of the four sleeves' daily unit returns over the
  **trailing 365 calendar days** before the month start (a sleeve needs ≥ 180 non-missing days; missing days = 0).
- Scale the ERC weights so the ex-ante portfolio vol is **10%/yr**, then apply caps (capital multiples):
  fxvol ≤ 3.0, btctrend ≤ 0.5, xmom ≤ 0.5, **fundcarry ≤ 0.5 (the funding-carry cap)**. Capped weights are not redistributed (as in the study).
- **Reweighting: monthly**, effective from the first UTC day of each month (first one 2026-11-01), using only returns before that day.
  The weights are constant capital multiples between reweights (daily return = Σ weight × sleeve unit return).
- History: the study's sleeve returns up to 2026-10-17 (copied into `sleeve_history.csv`) + the trial's live unit returns from
  2026-10-19 (`sleeve_returns.csv`). 2026-10-18 has no sleeve data and counts as 0, as the study's `fillna(0)` does.
- **Sleeve brake** (study): a sleeve is halved when its standalone drawdown (full history) exceeds 1.5× its trailing 365-day vol, and
  restored below 0.75×; evaluated at each daily mark, applied from the next day.
- **Portfolio brake** (study): all weights halved while the trial's drawdown from peak is > 10%, restored when it is back < 5%
  (checked before each day's return, on the previous mark).
- Entry weights = the study's October 2026 weights (trailing 365 days to 2026-09-30), recomputed by the runner from
  `sleeve_history.csv` and checked against the study (`rp_real_only` last weights: fxvol 1.614, btctrend 0.246, xmom 0.125, fundcarry 0.5).

## Entry
- At the **latest available close: 2026-10-19 00:00 UTC (05:30 IST)** = the 2026-10-18 UTC daily close for crypto (OKX 1Dutc).
  FX is entered at its latest completed close, Friday 2026-10-16 (FX markets were shut over the weekend; the Sunday-open gap is in
  Monday's return). The first P&L day is UTC 2026-10-19.
- Each sleeve starts in the position its own rule holds on that date (fxvol: the 2026-09-30 month-end decision; btctrend: the
  2026-10-14 Wednesday check; fundcarry: the 2026-10-18 Sunday check; xmom: the 2026-10-19 Monday open fill).
- Entry costs are charged up front at each sleeve's cost rate.

## Trial-level costs added on top of the study (conservative)
The study charges costs only inside sleeves. The trial also charges the cost of **changing a sleeve's capital** (entry, monthly
reweight, brake changes, halt): |Δ capital| × unit gross × rate, with fxvol 1 bp × 1.0, btctrend 17 bps × position, xmom 13 bps × 1.0
(5 bps fee + ~8 bps typical slippage of its book), fundcarry 19 bps × (hedged gross / 1.5). Daily drift between sleeves is not charged
(the study assumes constant weights).

## Risk limits
- **Hard halt: if equity at a daily mark is ≥ 15% below its running peak, close everything at that mark (with costs), status HALTED.**
  No restart without a new pre-registration. The study itself proposed no halt (its brakes are the 10%/5% portfolio brake and the
  sleeve brakes above, both kept); its worst backtest drawdown was 10.9%, so 15% is a backstop beyond the brake.
- Vol target 10%, caps and brakes as above. No discretionary overrides.

## Data (real only, never invented)

  monthly, forward-filled if FRED is unreachable.
- Crypto: OKX public REST with a User-Agent header (1Dutc candles for BTC-USDT, ETH-USDT spot and BTC/ETH-USDT-SWAP; funding-rate

What this means

What's real: FX vol basket (since 2010), BTC SMA100, crypto low-vol L/S and BTC/ETH funding carry pass the luck check. FX carry and multi-asset trend do not; trend lost money after costs in 2021–26. Combining helps mostly by cutting drawdowns and waiting time. The portfolio's Sharpe is about 1.5 against 1.1 for the best real single sleeve, its worst drop 11% against 12–15%, and every year since 2021 was positive. Be careful: three sleeves were picked on this same period, so expect roughly half (Sharpe 0.6–1.0, 5–9% a year at 10% vol). Without crypto the portfolio is weak (Sharpe 0.3). The funding carry's smooth Sharpe hides exchange-default risk, so it is capped at 50% of capital. A portfolio paper trial needs your approval.

System

Free data and free, open models, with no path to real money.

Architecture

data → signals → backtest → risk → paper broker, plus the AI discovery loop

How each part works

1
Data

Public price history only. Crypto: Binance's public data archive (daily history, including delisted coins) for research; OKX public candles via ccxt (Coinbase as a fallback) for the daily BTC paper run. The Binance trading API is blocked from this server's region. Forex: yfinance.

2
Signals

Rules that say what to buy or sell, either hand-written or proposed by the AI.

3
Backtest

Replays rules on past prices, including trading costs, using only information available at the time.

4
Risk

Position sizing, a drawdown stop and pre-trade checks.

5
Paper broker

A simulated account. The live-trading switch is hard-coded off; no keys and no wallets.

6
Discovery loop

An agent workflow on NVIDIA's NeMo Agent Toolkit. Nemotron (local via Ollama, or NVIDIA's free hosted API) proposes formulas from NVIDIA's Quantitative Signal Discovery blueprint, and we check them strictly. The model only ever sees training results.
